# Tandy 1000SL/2
The [Tandy 1000SL/2] was a mostly IBM PC-compatible machine launched in **1986**.

## Notes
- BIOS is non-configurable.
- Video card cannot be changed. Uses internal Tandy video card. Also supports TNDY sound card.
- Cannot boot from hard disk.
