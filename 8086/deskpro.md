# Compaq Deskpro
8086-based IBM PC-clone by Compaq. First in the Deskpro line.

## Notes
- BIOS is non-configurable.
- Designed to work with Compaq CGA/EGA cards. IBM CGA cards will not work. EGA cards and MDA cards will work.
