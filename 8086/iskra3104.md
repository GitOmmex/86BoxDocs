# Schetmash Iskra-3104
Russian IBM PC clone designed in **1989**. Developed by Elektronmash in Leningrad, Soviet Russia.

## Notes
- BIOS is non-configurable.
- May not work properly with an English keyboard, and is only partially compatible with ISO/IEC 8859-5
- BIOS will display graphical glitches unless used with an Iskra EGA card.
