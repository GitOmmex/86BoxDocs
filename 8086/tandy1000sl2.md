# Tandy 1000SL/2
8086 version of the iconic Tandy 1000.

## Notes
- BIOS is non-configurable.
- Video card cannot be changed. Uses internal Tandy video card. Uses internal TNDY sound card.
- Cannot boot from hard disk.
