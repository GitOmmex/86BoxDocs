# Tandy 1000 HX
Extremely popular XT clone. Has its own unique video and sound cards. This version supports booting from hard disk.

## Notes
- BIOS is non-configurable.
- Has internal TNDY video card and sound card. Comparable to Hercules and AdLib.
- Uniquely, this system has DOS 2.11 in ROM. If booted without a hard disk or floppy, it will boot from that.
